Why JanRakshak? The GovTech Paradigm Shift

Solving the fundamental paradox of disaster management applications.

❌ The Conventional Siloed System
  • • Municipal apps (potholes, garbage) and disaster helplines operate in completely isolated systems.
  • • When a tropical cyclone strikes, 98% of citizens have never downloaded the emergency app.
  • • Emergency hotlines get paralyzed by thousands of duplicate calls for the same fallen banyan tree or flooded culvert.
  • • Trapped bedridden elders with oxygen concentrators have no channel to signal neighborhood rescue boats.
✅ The JanRakshak Unified Dual-Mode
  • • 100% Pre-Crisis Adoption: Citizens use JanRakshak year-round for everyday civic grievances.
  • • Autonomic State Transformation: With one master Collector switch, all citizen apps switch into high-contrast crisis mode.
  • • 50m PostGIS Deduplication: Clusters duplicate complaints, scales priority, and stops dispatch paralysis.
  • • Targeted Vulnerability Mesh: Elder medical needs are pinned to pre-registered, verified volunteers within 500m.
